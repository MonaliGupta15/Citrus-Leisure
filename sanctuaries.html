5-Star Oceanfront Resort
KALUTARA COAST

Citrus Waskaduwa

Nestled on 10 acres of pristine coastline along Sri Lanka's golden west coast, Citrus Waskaduwa is an idyllic 5-star luxury sanctuary. Featuring 150 luxury ocean-facing rooms and suites, an expansive beachfront swimming pool, and the grand Mandarin Shore Ballroom accommodating up to 600 guests.

1835 Colonial Scottish Boutique
COLOMBO 01 FORT

The Steuart by Citrus

Step through history into the 1835 headquarters of George Steuart & Co, Ceylon's oldest mercantile establishment. Nestled in Colombo's heritage financial district, The Steuart blends Scottish colonial charm with contemporary 5-star executive luxury, featuring named county suites and the legendary &Co Pub and Kitchen.
