# Citrus Leisure PLC — 5-Star Luxury Hotels & Resorts Sri Lanka

An ultra-premium, responsive luxury hotel web platform unifying all four distinctive Citrus Leisure brand properties into a single, cohesive digital experience inspired by the Almaris luxury aesthetic.

---

## 🌴 Properties Unified
1. **Citrus Waskaduwa** — 5-Star Oceanfront Grandeur (Kalutara, Sri Lanka)
2. **Citrus Hikkaduwa** — Vibrant Coral Reef & Golden Surf Beach Retreat (Galle Coast)
3. **The Steuart by Citrus** — 1835 Scottish Heritage Boutique Hotel (&Co Pub & Kitchen, Colombo Fort)
4. **Citrus Leisure PLC** — Listed Hospitality Brand & Corporate Group

---

## ✨ Design & Architectural Highlights (Almaris Theme)
- **All-Screen Responsive**: Seamless layout adaptation across mobile (320px–480px), tablets, laptops (with collision-free navbar logic), and ultra-wide displays.
- **Glowing Golden Arch Hero Stage**: Signature luxury arch motif with active property switches and authentic background sliders.
- **Luxury Left Mandala Drawer**: Slide-out navigation drawer adorned with circular gold mandala filigree and direct access to all portals.
- **Floating Glass Booking Engine**: Real-time date validation, interactive guest stepper popover, and sanctuary selection.
- **Multi-Currency Live Engine**: Instant price conversion between **USD ($)**, **LKR (Rs)**, **GBP (£)**, **EUR (€)**, and **AUD (A$)**.
- **Triple Arched Room Showcase**: Category filter tabs (`All`, `Waskaduwa`, `Hikkaduwa`, `The Steuart`) with vertical arch-top photo frames and specs chips.
- **Hotel Facilities 6-Box Grid**: Cable TV, Smart Lock & Climate, King Size Bed, Free Fast Wi-Fi, Digital Safe, and Citron Senses Balinese Spa Access.
- **Gastronomy & Lounges**: Comprehensive showcase of &Co Pub and Kitchen, Lemon Sun All-Day Dining, Lemon Fish Seafood, and Summer Salt Beach Bar.
- **Citron Senses Balinese Spa**: Treatment menus, authentic therapy details, and instant ritual booking modal.
- **Instagram Photo Strip**: 6 authentic extracted photos with smooth zoom and gold Instagram badge overlays.

## 🚀 Getting Started
Open directly in any modern browser, or launch via a local web server:

```bash

# Open in browser
http://localhost:3000
```
